Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how otepad (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), provided by Gaurav Nelson, handles information when you use the App. otepad is an open-source, free note-taking application offered as-is.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Summary

otepad is designed to keep your notes on your device. We do not operate a backend that collects, stores, or sells your personal information or note content.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, or payment details through the App.

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Note titles and content you create
  • App preferences, such as light or dark theme

This information is saved in your browser or device storage (for example, local storage) and is not transmitted to us.

How we use information

Locally stored notes and preferences are used only to provide App features on your device, such as displaying your pads and remembering your theme choice. We do not use this information for advertising, analytics profiles, or resale.

Data storage and security

Because notes stay on your device, access to them depends on who can use that device and browser profile. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode, biometric lock, or similar security measure.

Clearing site data, uninstalling the App, or using “Delete all” (or deleting individual pads) removes stored notes from that browser or device profile. We cannot recover deleted local data.

Cookies and third-party services

The App does not use tracking cookies for advertising or analytics. It may use local browser storage as described above.

If you open the App from a hosted URL, your browser may load fonts or other static assets from third-party providers (for example, Google Fonts). Those providers may process technical request data according to their own policies. The App does not embed advertising SDKs or social tracking pixels.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
